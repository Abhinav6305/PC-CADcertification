    About the workshop

    Two days of hands-on power system simulation

    PSCAD (Power Systems Computer Aided Design) is an industry-standard tool for modelling electrical networks and studying how they behave in time. This two-day workshop took students from first login to confident circuit simulation — building circuits, running transient studies and reading waveforms, with every concept practised live on their own systems.

    Day 1 · 5 Oct

    Getting started with PSCAD

    Building a working foundation in the software environment.

    • Account creation, login, software download and setup
    • Navigating the workspace windows and project tree
    • What each toolbar button and core feature does
    • Exploring the master component libraries
    • Selecting components, wiring basic circuits and running first simulations
    Day 2 · 6 Oct

    RLC circuits & transient analysis

    Applying the fundamentals to real circuit behaviour.

    • Designing circuits with resistive, inductive and capacitive (RLC) elements
    • Transient analysis of voltage and current responses
    • Plotting and interpreting circuit parameters graphically
    • Comparing RL, RC and RLC combinations
    • Finding and fixing common simulation errors
    ⚡

    The closing challenge

    The workshop ended with a short technical competition: participants calculated circuit parameters by hand, then verified their answers against PSCAD’s simulated outputs. Two winners were selected based on the accuracy of their calculations and results.
